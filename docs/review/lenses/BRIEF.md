# Customer lenses — live-site brief (6 Sep 2026)

Objective: improve Piecebook from shopper and learner eyes **before the next phase**. Not Phase 10. Not 9.2 World rooms. No third wave of 20 “World-only” seats — every reviewer already lands on `/belong`.

Piecebook is unofficial. Quiet paper and ink. **Not a shop:** no cart, no checkout, no marketplace. Prices are seed (TCGplayer via Limitless / TCGCSV). Do not invent a buy button. “Shop” here means: find the card or box, see what it costs, know what it is, then buy it somewhere else.

Live home (start here): https://najibjuraimi-glitch.github.io/piecebook/
A first visit with nothing owned redirects `/` to `/start`. World is a tab and a door, not the assigned landing. No third wave of World-only seats.

## Collectr (shoppers review this first)
URL: https://app.getcollectr.com/?sortType=price&sortOrder=DESC&cardType=cards&category=68

What it actually is on 6 Sep 2026 (browser, no account):
- A multi-TCG **price tracker / collection app**, not a checkout.
- Header: Explore, Sets, Shop, Portfolio, Showcase. Search. Currency USD. Log in.
- One Piece cards, sort **Price highest → low**. Sidebar: product type Cards/Sealed, price range, language, category.
- Grid of card art. Many high prints carry a SAMPLE watermark. Name, set, number, foil, **large USD price**, green/red **% move**, a **+** on the tile.
- Phone: cart icon (0), same sort, two-up grid. Trophy / serial / manga prints first ($250,000 down).
- A newcomer sees the most expensive promos first. A player sees market chrome and % badges.

## Bandai how-to-play (learners review this first)
URL: https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/beginners/how-to-play.php

What it actually is on 6 Sep 2026:
- Official marketing funnel. Cookie wall on first load.
- Hero: “Learn the Rules to Play.” Then **tutorial video** and **READ THE GUIDE** — not five rules on the page.
- “This is all you need to start”: ST-01…ST-06 box photos, Learn Together set, VIEW MORE PRODUCTS.
- Official shop, Where to play, FAQ/manual pointers, **Tutorial App** (App Store / Google Play).
- Rules PDFs and videos live one click away (https://en.onepiece-cardgame.com/rules/): overview sheet, rule manual, comprehensive rules, tournament rules. The how-to-play **page itself does not teach** life, DON!!, attack, or the win.
- A newcomer is sold a starter deck and an app. A player who knows the anime still does not learn the TCG on this page alone.

## Piecebook — what is actually on the glass
Tabs: Sets · Collection · Decks · Portfolio · World.

**Start** `/start`: three doors — Collect / Play / World. “New to the game? Play has the rules in five sentences.” Unofficial line. “Just show me the sets.”

**World** `/belong`: cutoff chips (default Debut arc). Doors: Story, People, Fruits, The game → timeline. On This Day. Story index (titles and ranges, no plot). People/fruits log. The game rows: What you own → `/`, Sets in order → timeline, Play → `/decks`. Then “New to the game? Play has the rules in five sentences.” → `/learn`. **No box price and no card grid on this page.**

**Sets** `/`: search + facets. Tiles: box art, set name, card count, **box seed price**. Timeline view. Starter decks as rows. First visit to `/` with nothing owned redirects to Start here.

**Set detail** e.g. `/sets/OP-09`: set story (one sentence), dates, box as a product with seed price, card grid default **Price · high to low**, each cell seed $ and week move in ink. Star / own. No qty-add chrome.

**Learn** `/learn`: five sentences (leader + fifty + ten DON!!; turn; attack / life; counter / blocker / trigger; win). Then Bandai’s rules as outbound rows (rules page, overview PDF, manual, comprehensive, YouTube). Then Standard/Extra in one line → Decks.

**Decks** `/decks`: empty “No decks yet” + New deck. Link to Learn.

**Not here:** cart, checkout, official shop locator, teaching app, video on the page, fruit pictures, plot, wiki article links.
