# Start here — design seats (v3)

Jib, 6 Sep 2026, as a new tab: Collect / Play / World are *our* words. Collectr’s home is instantly a card shop. Bandai Asia (`https://asia-en.onepiece-cardgame.com/`) is instantly the One Piece TCG. Piecebook’s Start here does not say that, even to the person building it.

## The three questions a new browser must answer without knowing our pillars
1. Where do I find / shop for cards? (not “Collect”)
2. Where do I learn to play One Piece TCG? (not “Play”)
3. Where do I read about characters and fruits? (not “World”)

## Hard constraints
- Quiet paper / ink. One accent only on a primary action — **no accent on Start doors**.
- **Not a shop.** No cart, checkout, buy button, Collectr `%` / SAMPLE / + chrome.
- No wiki images. No official fruit art. No invented plot.
- Art we may use: TCGplayer box CDN (already on Sets tiles), Limitless card CDN (already on card cells). Example prints: OP-01 / OP-16 box, ST-08 or ST-21 starter box, `OP01-003` Monkey.D.Luffy.
- Tab labels stay. Route `/belong` stays. World remains the house name in chrome.
- Do not turn Piecebook into Collectr or Bandai’s store.
