# C02 — Gift buyer, cheapest booster box (new to One Piece)

**Reference first impression**
Collectr opens One Piece as Cards Only, Price high to low: trophy and serial prints at six figures, SAMPLE on the art, Shop in the header, a + on every tile. I came for a box. The first screen is unbuyable singles. Sealed Only is a sidebar tap I would not know to make.

**Piecebook home / Start first impression**
A first visit opens Start: Collect / Play / World. Collect is “what you have and what it is worth today.” I have nothing and I am buying a gift. “Just show me the sets” is the last line. No box art on this page.

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — Collect does not say find a box. It sends a first visit back through “what you have.” The cheap-box job is a last-line escape.
2. High — Sets tiles are oldest EN first. The first product is Romance Dawn, Box · US $1468.78. The cheapest released EN box in the roster (OP-16, $207.42) sits after a long stack of $300–$600 boxes.
3. High — I do not know OP-16 from OP-01. Nothing on the first fold says “lowest box seed” or “newest box.” Jump links go to coming soon and starter decks, not cheapest sealed.
4. Med — Timeline (“Sets in order”) has dates and no box figure, so the “order” door is the one without a gift sticker.
5. Med — Starter-deck rows under the tiles look like cheaper gifts (~the live seed on those rows) but they are not booster boxes. I can buy the wrong product.

**3 that work**
- Every released booster tile prints box art and `Box · US $… · as of` — once I find OP-16 I can take a dated seed to a shop.
- Start names TCGplayer and “Read how prices work.” I am not told a fake number.
- No cart, no SAMPLE, no +. Quiet paper.

**One trade**
Oldest-first history (the English print run) vs putting the lowest current box seed on the first fold for a gift buyer.

**One improvement**
On Sets, add a jump to the released booster tile with the lowest box seed (today that is OP-16 at $207.42) so a cheapest-box gift does not start at Romance Dawn.
