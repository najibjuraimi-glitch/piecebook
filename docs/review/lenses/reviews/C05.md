# C05 — Teen, $20, phone only (new to One Piece)

**Reference first impression**
On the phone it opens like a shop — search, filters, a two-up grid, a + on every tile — but the first One Piece cards are trophy and serial prints at six figures ($200,000 Kaido, $100,000 Yamato, then more SAMPLE-watermarked promos). With $20 I cannot tell what a normal card costs; I only learn that this hobby is for people who already won.

**Piecebook /belong first impression**
World is a reading desk, not a first buy: “How far have you read,” Debut-arc chips, Story / People / Fruits, On This Day (Gecko Moria’s birthday). There is no box sticker and no card grid. “The game” is three text doors (What you own, Sets in order, Play) plus “New to the game? Play has the rules in five sentences” — I still do not see anything I can spend $20 on.

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — Reviewers land on `/belong`. That page cannot do the job: no price, no box, no single. I have to guess that Sets is the shop list.
2. High — Sets tiles lead with Romance Dawn Box · US $1,468.78, then $588 / $525 and more $200–$600 boxes. Same Collectr feeling: the first sticker says I cannot play.
3. High — OP-09 (and set grids) default to Price · high to low. First cells are thousand-dollar manga/alts; the $0.36–$0.43 cards exist only after a very long phone scroll.
4. Med — The $16–$19 starter-deck rows sit under every booster and the ~$400 coming-soon boxes. The header jump “36 starter decks” helps only if I already know that phrase means “the $20 thing.”
5. Med — I do not know One Piece. World asks how far I have read; set copy names Roger / Shanks / Lim; starter rows are “ST-35 · RED/BLACK Sabo.” On OP-09 many mid-grid cells are empty art placeholders.

**3 that work**
- Set tiles and the OP-09 box card state the product in ink: box art, pack line, TCGplayer market, dated seed.
- Sets already names the cheap sealed path: 23 booster sets, 2 coming soon, then 36 starter decks, and those rows carry a seed price in my range.
- Start’s three doors plus Just show me the sets, and Belong’s line to Play in five sentences — quiet paper, no SAMPLE chrome, no +.

**One trade**
Trade World-as-the-first-screen (and high-to-low as the first card sort) for one affordable product on the first phone fold — a starter-deck row or Price · low to high when I own nothing.

**One improvement**
On `/belong` under The game, put one starter-deck row that is at or under $20 (the rows Sets already has) so a phone-only first buy sees a product, a dated seed price, and a path to its set before any $1,400 box.
