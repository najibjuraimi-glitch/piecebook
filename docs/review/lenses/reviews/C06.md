# C06 — Singapore, S$, first buy (new to One Piece)

**Reference first impression**
Collectr opens as a One Piece singles grid already sorted Price high to low, so the first tiles are trophy and serial prints at $200,000 / $100,000 / $100,000, many stamped SAMPLE, each with a large USD figure, a 0.00% badge, and a +. The header is locked to $ USD; language (English / Japanese / Chinese) and price range are PRO — nothing on the first screen is a first buy I can pay for in S$.

**Piecebook /belong first impression**
World asks how far I have read, defaults to Debut arc, and offers Story, People, Fruits, and The game — plus Gecko Moria’s birthday and “Sets in English order.” There is no card, no box, no dollar, and no S$; it is a spoiler-safe English book, not a place to make a first purchase.

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — `/belong` has no search, no seed price, no card, and no box. The URL I was sent to cannot start “buy a card or box.”
2. High — World and Sets default US $. The only `US $` / `S$` chips sit on Portfolio. A first buyer with nothing owned opens an empty book (“No owned cards yet”) — S$ is a collector reading, not a shopping one.
3. High — Sets is EN boxes: Romance Dawn Box · US $1,468.78, then US $588 / $525 / $1,081. Same shock as Collectr’s trophies. The first-buy-sized starter rows (ST-08 Monkey.D.Luffy US $18.40, ST-03 US $18.50) sit under that stack as text.
4. Med — OP-09 leads with the EN booster box at a bare $669.52 (TCGplayer market · as of 4 Sep 2026), then a card grid defaulted to Price · high to low: manga Roger $5,500 / manga Luffy $2,699. I do not know those names. The unit is `$`, not S$, and not even the “US $” the tiles use.
5. Med — Completely new to One Piece, non-English-market: World assumes a reading cutoff; The game says “Sets in English order”; every tile is EN set. Collectr at least tags some JP / CN prints (and locks language behind PRO). Piecebook’s catalog is US-market English product. I still take a US seed to a shop myself.

**3 that work**
- Set tiles print `Box · US $… · as of 4 Sep 2026` — dated, prefixed US $, I can take that number to a shop and I am not told it is S$.
- The OP-09 box is a product (EN booster box, TCGplayer market, dated seed) with a card grid of seed $ and week move. No +, no cart.
- Portfolio already has `US $` / `S$` chips (dated European Central Bank reading, not a local ask). The switch exists; Start also names TCGplayer and “Read how prices work.”

**One trade**
Collectr puts cards and a USD price on the first screen (only the unbuyable ones); Piecebook can show a dated EN box seed, but S$ only after I already own cards, and the landing URL is an English story room.

**One improvement**
On Sets, put the existing Portfolio `US $` / `S$` chips on the box seed so a first Singapore buy can read US $669.52 as S$ before they own anything.
