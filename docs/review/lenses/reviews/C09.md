# C09 — Sealed box gift, afraid of fake prices (new to One Piece)

**Reference first impression**
Collectr opens like a shop — Find a Product, Shop, a cart — then One Piece as Cards Only, Price high to low, so the first glass is SAMPLE-watermarked trophies at $200,000 / $100,000 / $100,000. I came to give a sealed box and not get cheated; those numbers look stamped, and Sealed Only is a sidebar radio I would not know to press.

**Piecebook home / Start first impression**
Start is three text doors and the first honest line I have seen: “Nothing here is invented: prices from TCGplayer, cards from Limitless, dates from Bandai. Read how prices work.” There is still no box on this page; Collect is “what you have,” and the gift lives behind the last line, “Just show me the sets.”

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — I tap how prices work before I tap a door. `/about-prices` says every figure is a seed (named source, stated date, TCGCSV for English boxes), never a live quote, never invented, no marketplace or affiliate. That is the job. It still does not show a box I can copy and take to a shop.
2. High — Sets is oldest EN first. The first tile is Romance Dawn, Box · US $1,468.78 · as of 4 Sep 2026. I do not know One Piece. That sticker looks like Collectr’s fantasy tape, not a birthday box.
3. High — Tiles print `Box · US $… · as of` and stop. The words that would kill the fake-price fear — “TCGplayer market” — sit on `/about-prices` and on the OP-09 box card. I have to leave the list to believe the list.
4. Med — World has no search, no box, no dollar. Timeline (`/?view=timeline`) has dates and no figure. Learn is five sentences; Decks is “No decks yet.” The wrong door loses the sticker I came to trust.
5. Med — I do not know OP-09 from a shop box. Search is “Search cards, sets, artists.” Coming-soon tiles then show Box · pre-order US $399.72 · as of 4 Sep 2026, and 36 starter-deck rows sit under the stack; I can copy the wrong product.

**3 that work**
- Start names the sources and links `/about-prices`. The unofficial line is on the same fold. I know this is not a checkout and no one is paid when I buy elsewhere.
- Every released booster tile I opened prints box art and a dated seed (OP-01 US $1,468.78, OP-02 US $588.05, OP-09 US $669.52, all as of 4 Sep 2026). No SAMPLE, no +, no cart.
- OP-09’s box card is the product I wanted: EN booster box, $669.52, TCGplayer market · as of 4 Sep 2026, 24 packs. I can screenshot that line and walk into a shop. The card grid under it (Price · high to low, Manga Art $5,500 / $2,699) is not the gift.

**One trade**
Collectr’s shop chrome and SAMPLE $200,000 tiles vs Piecebook’s dated seed I can take to a real counter — the method and the first sticker are two rooms apart, and the first sticker is Romance Dawn.

**One improvement**
On Sets, put the existing “Read how prices work” line above the first booster tile so a sealed-gift buyer can check the source on the same page as the dated Box · US $ seed.
