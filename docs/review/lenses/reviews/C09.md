**Piecebook home / Start first impression**
Start says nothing is invented: TCGplayer, Limitless, Bandai. “Read how prices work.” That is the first honest line I have seen. Collect still says “what you have.” I have a gift to buy.

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — I need a box seed I can trust. The first Sets tile is Romance Dawn at $1468.78. I cannot tell if that is a normal gift or a grail.
2. Med — How prices work is under the doors. I want it next to the box sticker.
3. Med — The box card on OP-09 names TCGplayer market and a date. The tile says `as of`. Good. The TCGPlayer URL is not a link (by design). I cannot tap through to the listing.
4. Med — Collectr and Piecebook both default chase-first on cards. For a sealed gift I should stay on tiles, but I do not know that.
5. Low — S$ is only on Portfolio. A gift in Singapore is still a US seed.

**3 that work**
- Dated `Box · US $… · as of` on tiles. I can take that to a shop and compare.
- About prices names the seed. I am told this is not a live checkout.
- Unofficial line. I know who is not selling me the box.

**One trade**
A tap-through to TCGplayer (we do not do) vs a dated seed I can read in a shop.
