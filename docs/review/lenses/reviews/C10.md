# C10 — Visual shopper, must see the box (new to One Piece)

**Reference first impression**
Collectr opens as Cards Only, Price high to low: a wall of card art, many stamped SAMPLE, Kaido at $200,000 then Yamato and Luffy at $100,000, Shop in the header. I came to match a box I saw in a shop window; Sealed Only is a sidebar tap I would miss, and the first glass is singles, not a box face.

**Piecebook home / Start first impression**
A first visit to `/` sends me to Start: Collect / Play / World, all text, no picture of a box. Collect is “what you have and what it is worth today”; “Just show me the sets” is the last line — the only door that can start “I will know it when I see the art.”

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — Start has no box art. The job is match a face I already saw. I cannot start on the first Piecebook page.
2. High — World (the third door, and `/belong`) has no box, no seed, no search. “Sets in order” opens Timeline: dates and JP titles, no art, no `Box · US $`.
3. Med — Sets tiles are oldest EN first. Romance Dawn leads, Box · US $1,468.78 · as of 4 Sep 2026. I do not know OP-01 from a current shop box; the face I might actually recognize sits after a long stack of names I cannot read.
4. Med — Search is “Search cards, sets, artists.” I do not know a code. Starter-deck rows under the tiles use a 48px thumb — too small to match a deck box I saw on a shelf.
5. Low — OP-09, once I tap a tile I recognize, still leads with the box, then a card grid defaulted to Price · high to low (Manga Art $5,500). I already found the box; the chase wall is not the job.

**3 that work**
- Released booster tiles show the official TCGplayer box render, EN SET · code, name, card count, and `Box · US $… · as of` — that is the window match, and a dated seed I can take to a shop.
- OP-09’s box card is a large official render: EN booster box, $669.52, TCGplayer market · as of 4 Sep 2026, 24 packs, no cart, no +.
- No SAMPLE watermark on the box photos. Quiet paper. I can screenshot the face and the sticker and buy the same product somewhere else.

**One trade**
Collectr puts pictures on the first screen (the wrong pictures: cards, not boxes). Piecebook has the box wall I need, but only after Start’s three text doors.

**One improvement**
On Start, after the doors, show the existing newest-released booster tile (box art, name, `Box · US $… · as of`) so I can match a shop-window box without knowing Romance Dawn.
