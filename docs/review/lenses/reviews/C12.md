**Reference first impression**
Collectr is already my chase tape: Price high to low, One Piece, Cards Only. Trophy and serial first, SAMPLE on a lot of the art, a large USD and a % move. I can see the top of the market in one scroll. Language and price-range sit in the sidebar (some of that is PRO).

**Piecebook home / Start first impression**
Start is a first-run desk, not a chase desk. Collect is “what you have.” I already play. I tap Sets. I am not on manga Luffy yet.

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — There is no all-OP chase grid. Singles are set-scoped. I must open OP-09 (or search a name) to see manga / SEC. Collectr’s first glass is the whole market.
2. High — Sets tiles are boxes. The chase lives one tap deeper, then the set grid does default Price · high to low — that part matches the job, but only inside one set.
3. Med — Serial / trophy / championship prints that Collectr leads with are often promos outside the EN booster tiles. Piecebook’s EN roster will not show a $200,000 Kid trophy as a first cell.
4. Med — Search is “cards, sets, artists,” not “manga” or “serial.” Variant names exist on the card (`Manga Art`) but I cannot facet them from Sets home.
5. Low — Start redirect on an empty collection wastes a tap I do not need.

**3 that work**
- OP-09’s default Price · high to low puts manga / SEC at the top of that set, seed $ and week move in ink, no SAMPLE chrome.
- Card cells name the variant. I can tell Manga Art from the common.
- Quiet paper, dated seed, star / own only. I take the number to a shop.

**One trade**
One global high-to-low chase tape vs one honest EN-set grid that cannot list every promo Collectr shows.

**One improvement**
On Sets search, treat the existing variant word (`Manga Art`, `Serial`) as a facet so a chase query is not “open OP-09 and hope.”
