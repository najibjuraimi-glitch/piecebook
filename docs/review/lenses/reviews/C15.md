# C15 — Deck builder filling a list gap (plays One Piece)

**Reference first impression**
Collectr opens as a One Piece singles grid already sorted Price high to low, so the first tiles are trophy, serial, and promo prints at $200,080 / $100,008 / $100,000, many stamped SAMPLE, each with a large USD figure, a green or red move, and a +. Search, Cards Only, and the category rail are there, but a player with a fifty-card list is looking at market chrome for chase, not the cheap numbers they still need.

**Piecebook /belong first impression**
World is a reader page: “How far have you read,” Debut arc selected, doors for Story, People, Fruits, and The game, then On This Day (6 Sep, Gecko Moria). There is no card, no seed price, and no list on this screen; Play sits under The game as “Build a deck from your cards,” which is the first line that even points at the job.

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — Reviewers land on `/belong`. A gap buyer has to scroll past cutoff chips and wiki rooms to reach Play, then leave World entirely. The job (priced missing singles) is not on the first glass.
2. High — Play’s note is “Build a deck from your cards.” The job is a list from elsewhere, then the holes. That line sends you into the collection, not into paste-a-list.
3. High — Sets is boxes: art, name, card count, box seed price. Opening OP-09 still leads with the box as a product, then a card grid defaulted to Price · high to low — the same chase-first stack as Collectr, not “the four copies this list still needs.”
4. Med — Decks home is empty: “No decks yet,” then New deck. The useful sentence (“paste a list… see what you already own”) is on that card, but the missing prints and their seed dollars are not on this page; you have to start a deck before any gap exists.
5. Med — World has no card search. A 50-card list spans sets. From `/belong` the path is Sets → pick a set → find a name. That is a sealed catalog walk, not a list check.

**3 that work**
1. The Decks empty card already names the job in words: paste a list, add fifty, see what you own — and New deck is the one orange action.
2. OP-09 cells print seed $ and the week move in ink, with star / own only. No + tile, no cart. Enough to take a number to a shop.
3. World’s Play row does land on `/decks`, and Sets still shows the box as a priced product if the list gap is actually a sealed hole.

**One trade**
Keep World as the quiet book reviewers are sent to, versus putting a paste-list / missing-singles view on the first screen a player with a list actually needs.

**One improvement**
On `/belong`, change Play to the Decks empty-card words (“paste a list… see what you already own”) and open that paste field so the next screen is the missing prints with seed $ — not a story door and not a box grid.
