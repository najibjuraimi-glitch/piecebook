**Piecebook home / Start first impression**
A first visit to `/` sends me to Start: Collect / Play / World, the TCGplayer / Limitless / Bandai trust line, and “Read how prices work.” That is the honest sentence I came for — a seed, not a live quote — but there is no search, no card, no box, and no `as of` on this page. World (`/belong`) is worse for the job: Debut arc, Story, People, Fruits, The game, 6 Sep / Gecko Moria, and still no sticker.

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — I already know the name on the case. Search (“Search cards, sets, artists”) lives on Sets, not on Start or World. Until I leave these pages I cannot type the sticker and get a dated seed.
2. High — A first visit to Sets with nothing owned redirects to Start (Collect / Play / World). I play; I just have not marked cards. Extra doors before any `Box · US $… · as of`.
3. High — OP-09 cells print seed $ and the week move in ink, not `as of`. The date that makes a seed a sticker I can read to the clerk lives one tap deeper on the card Market block. Sets search’s exact-number row says Open and has no $ at all.
4. Med — The game → “Sets in order” opens the Timeline: EN dates, no box figure. I need price and date on the same line. That line is already on the tiles, not on this catalog-shaped door.
5. Med — OP-09’s grid defaults to Price · high to low, same chase-first stack as Collectr. Manga / SEC lead; the staples a local case actually stickers sit under that wall. Week move is on those cells once I scroll.

**3 that work**
1. Set tiles print `Box · US $… · as of 4 Sep 2026` (OP-09 also `Box · S$750` over `US $669.52 · as of 4 Sep 2026`) — a dated sealed seed I can put next to the shop’s box sticker.
2. The card Market block prints seed $ and `as of` in ink. How prices work names it a seed from TCGplayer, not a live quote and not a shop ask. No +, no cart, no SAMPLE.
3. Once I reach Sets, search matches a name or a number and facets are not PRO — type the case, open the card, read the dated seed, buy it at the shop.

**One trade**
Collectr puts a dollar and a % on the first card so I start comparing immediately, but it is a $208,080 trophy with no as-of; Piecebook’s dated seed is the number I can defend at the counter, but only after I leave Start / World, and the date is on the box tile and the card page, not on the cell I would screenshot.
