# P01 — Board gamer, never watched One Piece (learn)

**Reference first impression**
Bandai’s how-to-play is a red marketing page: cookie wall, “Learn the Rules to Play,” a tutorial video and READ THE GUIDE, then starter-deck photos, official shop, where to play, and a teaching app. I came for the rules of a new game. This page does not tell me how a turn works.

**Piecebook home / Start first impression**
Start is quiet. Play says “Learn the game in five sentences, build a deck from your cards.” That door opens Decks: “No decks yet” and New deck. The five sentences are a second link under the doors.

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — The Play door goes to an empty deck list, not Learn. A board gamer who taps Play still has not learned a turn.
2. High — I do not know what a leader or DON!! is until I find `/learn`. Start does not show even the first sentence on the first fold.
3. Med — After I reach Learn, the five sentences are dense. They do teach draw, DON!!, attack, life, counter, blocker, trigger, and the win — which Bandai’s landing page does not.
4. Med — The next step on Learn is outbound PDFs and YouTube, or Decks. There is no “play a first game with a starter” using the starter rows Sets already has.
5. Low — World asks how far I have read. I have not. That door is a side path for this job.

**3 that work**
- `/learn` actually states the rules in words. Bandai’s how-to-play page does not.
- Terms are defined in the sentences (DON!! as currency, rested as turned sideways, life into hand).
- Bandai’s overview sheet and manual are one tap away, labelled as PDF.

**One trade**
Bandai’s video + app funnel vs Piecebook’s five sentences that I can finish on a phone without a cookie wall.

**One improvement**
Make the Play door open `/learn`, not empty Decks, so the first Play tap is the five sentences.
