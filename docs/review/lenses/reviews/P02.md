# P02 — Pokémon player switching (learn, new to One Piece)

**Reference first impression**
Bandai’s how-to-play looks like a product launch: video, beginner’s guide, six starter boxes, official shop, app. As a Pokémon player I know “buy a starter, watch a video.” I still do not know how this game’s turn differs from a Pokémon turn.

**Piecebook home / Start first impression**
Play promises five sentences then a deck. The door is Decks, empty. Learn is the underlined sentence under the doors. Sets looks like a card database I already understand once I get there.

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — I tap Play and get New deck, not the rules. In Pokémon I would have expected a How to Play article first.
2. Med — Learn’s five sentences are one block. I want Energy vs DON!!, Prize vs life, in two columns. Piecebook will not invent a Pokémon glossary, so I have to map it myself.
3. Med — Sets is boxes and a card grid. Useful once I know a leader. The learn path does not point at one starter row as “your first sixty” — here, fifty plus a leader.
4. Med — Bandai’s page at least shows starter box photos. Piecebook Learn has no product picture.
5. Low — Standard / Extra at the foot of Learn is format talk I do not need on night one.

**3 that work**
- Five sentences name the win, the first-turn skip, and DON!! as the spend. That is the difference I came for.
- Starter-deck rows on Sets are the “theme deck” I already know how to buy.
- No cookie wall, no app install to read the rules.

**One trade**
A video and a box photo (Bandai) vs five accurate sentences and a priced starter row one tab away (Piecebook).

**One improvement**
Under Learn’s five sentences, link the existing starter-deck group on Sets (“Ready-made decks Bandai sells”) so a Pokémon switcher sees the first product after the rules.
