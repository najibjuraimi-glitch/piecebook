# P03 — Parent playing with a kid (learn, new to One Piece)

**Reference first impression**
Bandai’s page is loud: cookies, video, shop, app. I can show a kid the box photos. I cannot read the rules to them off this page.

**Piecebook home / Start first impression**
Quiet paper. “Five sentences.” Play opens Decks — a kid will tap New deck. Learn is the page I would read aloud.

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — Play → New deck. A child will not find the sentences.
2. High — Five sentences are long to read aloud. No picture of the table.
3. Med — Starter decks (the thing I would actually buy a kid) sit under expensive boxes.
4. Med — World cutoff is a manga spoiler tool. A kid who watches the show will tap it.
5. Low — PDFs are not a bedtime read.

**3 that work**
- `/learn` is short enough to try.
- Unofficial line I can explain.
- Starter rows exist once I reach Sets.

**One trade**
A video I would play on the sofa (Bandai) vs five sentences I can read (Piecebook).

**One improvement**
Play door → `/learn`, and a jump from Learn to starter decks, so a parent buys the $20-class product after the rules, not a $1400 box.
