# P03 — Parent playing with a kid (learn, new to One Piece)

**Reference first impression**
Bandai’s how-to-play is a cookie wall, then “Learn the Rules to Play,” a tutorial video, READ THE GUIDE, ST-01…ST-06 box photos, an official shop, and a Tutorial App. I sat down with a child to learn enough to teach; this page never says life, DON!!, attack, or the win.

**Piecebook home / Start first impression**
Start is quiet paper: Play promises five sentences, then “build a deck from your cards,” but that door opens empty Decks and New deck. The five sentences are the smaller underlined line under the doors — the tap I want before a child sits down.

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — Play opens an empty deck list. I cannot teach a turn from “Pick a leader or paste a list.” We own nothing.
2. High — After I find Learn, the next ink is Bandai PDFs, YouTube, Standard/Extra, and “Ready to build? Decks.” A first game with a child needs two boxes on the table, not a builder and not rotation.
3. Med — The five sentences do teach the turn and the win. They are adult-long. Sentence two is draw, two DON!!, spend, first-player skip, and no first-turn attack in one breath. I can study it. I cannot say it to a kid as written.
4. Med — Bandai already showed ST-01…ST-06 as “all you need to start.” Sets has those starter rows, under booster tiles that lead with Romance Dawn at Box · US $1468.78. Home → Learn never points at the two boxes we need.
5. Low — World asks how far we have read. We have not. Debut arc is safe later for “who is on this card.” It does not teach an attack tonight.

**3 that work**
- `/learn` names leader, DON!! as currency, life into hand, counter / blocker / trigger, and the win. Bandai’s landing page does not.
- The overview sheet is labelled “PDF · one page, the fastest read.” I can print that for the table.
- No cookie wall, no app install, no shop. I can prep on a phone while the kid waits.

**One trade**
Bandai’s video, teaching app, and starter photos vs Piecebook’s five sentences I can finish. I gain rules I can study. I still have to hunt Sets for the two boxes we sit down with.

**One improvement**
Change Learn’s closer from “Ready to build? Decks” to the existing Sets jump to starter decks, worded for two players (you and the child), so the next tap after the five sentences is two boxes, not New deck.
