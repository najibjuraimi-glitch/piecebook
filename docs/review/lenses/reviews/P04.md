# P04 — Absolute beginner, what is a leader (learn, new to One Piece)

**Reference first impression**
Bandai says learn the rules, then shows a video tile and a guide tile. I do not know what a leader is. The page never says it. It shows starter boxes and an app.

**Piecebook home / Start first impression**
Start’s Play line mentions “build a deck from your cards” and “five sentences.” I still do not see a leader. Learn’s first sentence is the first time the word is used: “a leader and a deck of fifty cards in the leader’s colours.”

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — Leader is defined only as something you bring. I do not see one. I do not know it is a card with life that sits on the table.
2. High — Play opens Decks. New deck asks me to pick a leader before I know what one is.
3. Med — Set grids show Leader rarity on a deck page, but I have to open a starter set to see a leader print.
4. Med — World’s People list is “printed Character and Leader,” which is a collector word, not a rules word.
5. Low — The Play block on a card (category Leader) exists only after I open a card I cannot yet name.

**3 that work**
- Sentence 1 names leader, fifty, colours, and ten DON!!.
- A starter-deck page files the leader first and has a Leader tab.
- Bandai’s rule manual is linked if I want a picture they already published.

**One trade**
Keep Learn as five sentences vs adding one more sentence that says a leader is the card that holds your life and stays on the table.

**One improvement**
Add one clause to sentence 1: the leader stays in play and holds your life, so “what is a leader” is answered before New deck asks me to pick one.
