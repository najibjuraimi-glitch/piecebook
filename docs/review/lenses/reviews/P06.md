# P06 — Text rules, not video (learn, new to One Piece)

**Reference first impression**
Bandai’s how-to-play is a video funnel: cookie wall, “Learn the Rules to Play,” then a TUTORIAL VIDEO tile and READ THE GUIDE — the page itself never writes life, DON!!, attack, or the win. Scroll and it sells starter decks, an official shop, WHERE TO PLAY, and a Tutorial App; the words I want live one click away on the rules PDF page, still behind the cookie wall.

**Piecebook home / Start first impression**
Start is quiet paper. Play’s door says “Learn the game in five sentences” — words on a page, which is the job — then opens Decks: “No decks yet” and New deck. The sentences sit on `/learn`, reached by a smaller line under the doors.

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — Play (and World’s Play row) goes to an empty deck list, not `/learn`. I came to read and got New deck.
2. High — On Learn, BANDAI'S RULES leads with the official rules page, noted as “how to play, tutorial videos and every document below.” I just left Bandai because it pushed video; the overview sheet and rule manual (PDF · one page / full starter rulebook) sit under that.
3. Med — The five sentences are not on Start. I have to notice the second link, or find Learn from Decks.
4. Med — Official channel (YouTube · Bandai's how-to-play videos) sits as a peer of the PDFs. I will not press play.
5. Low — World asks how far I have read. I have not read or watched One Piece. Sets and OP-09 are box stickers and a price grid — not a lesson.

**3 that work**
- `/learn` prints the rules in five sentences on the page: setup, turn, attack/life, counter/blocker/trigger, the win. Bandai’s how-to-play page does not.
- Those sentences define their own terms in words (DON!! as currency, rested as turned sideways, counter, blocker, Trigger).
- The overview sheet and rule manual are already outbound rows, labelled as PDFs — the text path Bandai hid behind a video tile and an app.

**One trade**
Bandai’s video + app + product page vs Piecebook’s five sentences and labelled PDFs on one quiet phone screen. I lose official pictures and a teaching app; I gain words I can finish without pressing play.

**One improvement**
Lead BANDAI'S RULES with the overview sheet Piecebook already labels “PDF · one page, the fastest read,” so the tap after the five sentences is a page of words, not a row that advertises tutorial videos.
