# P07 — Confused by DON!! and life (new to One Piece)

**Reference first impression**
Bandai’s how-to-play is a cookie wall, then “Learn the Rules to Play,” a tutorial video, READ THE GUIDE, starter-deck photos, official shop, and a Tutorial App. I came holding two words — DON!! and life — and this page never prints either.

**Piecebook home / Start first impression**
Start is three quiet doors. Play promises “Learn the game in five sentences” and opens empty Decks; the line under the doors is the path to `/learn`, where DON!! is named as currency and life as cards in a pile.

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — Neither Bandai nor Start prints DON!! or life. I only meet the two words on `/learn`, and the Play door does not go there.
2. High — Play opens “No decks yet” and New deck. I still do not know what either word means.
3. Med — On Learn, DON!! sits inside sentence 1 and life inside sentence 3. No labels. I have to parse a full turn to find the two words I brought.
4. Med — World is Story / People / Fruits and a timeline. “Life” there sounds like the manga. No DON!!.
5. Low — Learn’s next step is Bandai PDFs and YouTube — the same official funnel that hid the words — or Decks.

**3 that work**
- Sentence 1: ten DON!! cards, “the currency that pays for everything.”
- Sentence 3: a hit on the leader moves “one life card from their life pile into their hand” — life is cards, not hit points.
- Sentence 5: you win when they have “no life cards left,” so I know why the pile matters.

**One trade**
Bandai’s shop-and-app page never defines the two words; Piecebook defines both in two clauses, but only after I find `/learn`.

**One improvement**
On `/learn`, put the existing DON!!-as-currency and life-pile clauses as two labeled words at the top, so I can find those terms without reading a full turn.
