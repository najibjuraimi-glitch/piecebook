# P16 — Wants to teach a newbie (learn, plays One Piece)

**Reference first impression**
Bandai’s page is what I would send a newbie — and they will bounce on cookies, video, and shop. The teaching app might help if they install it.

**Piecebook home / Start first impression**
I would send `/learn`. It is five sentences and Bandai links. I would not send Start, because Play opens Decks. I would not send World.

**Top 5 frictions**
1. High — The URL I want to text is `/learn`, but the site’s Play door does not go there. A newbie I send to home will tap Play and see New deck.
2. Med — No board picture I can point at across the table.
3. Med — Five sentences are a lot to read aloud. P10’s split-lines would help me teach.
4. Low — Starter product is on Sets, not under Learn, so “buy this then read that” is two URLs.
5. Low — World will distract a manga-curious newbie.

**3 that work**
- `/learn` is linkable and short enough to read together.
- Official PDFs sit under the sentences when they ask “is that real.”
- No marketplace chrome while I am teaching.

**One trade**
One teach URL vs Bandai’s app, which I cannot put on the table as text.

**One improvement**
Play door → `/learn`, and put one starter-deck jump under the sentences, so the URL I text is home or Learn and both reach the same first lesson.
